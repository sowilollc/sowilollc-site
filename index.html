Atachy

Atachy is a dating app built on attachment science. A free 12-question assessment shows how you tend to act when closeness gets complicated, and Rowan, Atachy's AI relationship coach, helps you work with that while you date.

No games, no pay-to-be-seen visibility. Just people who'd rather fix the pattern than swipe to the next one.
